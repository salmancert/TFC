How the estimate is built

  • Air ticket — route history, re-anchored to live market fares when available.
  • Accommodation — destination nightly rate × nights.
  • Daily allowance — policy rate × days. Exact, not predicted.
  • Other — incidental spend per day for the destination.

Each component is fitted two ways — a hierarchical route model and a gradient boosted tree — and the one that wins on held-out folds is kept. Components marked boosted found structure the simpler model could not express.
